## Concept
- **The title joke:** the title is itself Denglish. *Englisch für Fortgeschrittene* means "English for advanced learners", but *fort-geschritten* literally means "away-stepped", hence "runaways".
- **The story:** a deadpan spaghetti-western ballad about Jürgen the outlaw, told entirely in Denglish. It ends with him riding off on his *wire donkey* (*Drahtesel*, a bicycle) into the *sun-under-going* (*Sonnenuntergang*, sunset).
- **Staying fresh:** the two entries the OP praised were Oktoberfest oompah-punk and happy hardcore. Both are idiom lists, and this song avoids every idiom they used.
